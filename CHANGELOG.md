# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release

- trace the selected board image as you see it, crop and rotation included, or an uploaded, dropped or pasted one, into native pen strokes
- Outline traces every shape and hole; Centerline follows the middle of each line; Colour layers outlines 2 to 8 colours, each in its own colour
- threshold with Auto, invert, detail tolerance, specks, smooth curves, stroke width, colour and working resolution, with a live preview and stroke and point counts
- a photo starts in Colour layers unless you pick a mode
- places the strokes as one group beside the source image, or in the middle of your view, at the first free spot, selects them and undoes in one step
- ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z work from the panel, and the panel follows the board theme live
- asks for storage and board access on first open, not at page load
- settings are remembered between sessions
